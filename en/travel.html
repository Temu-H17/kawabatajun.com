Travel & Railways

Railways & travel

I enjoy railways not only as a means of transportation but also as a way to observe regions, cities, infrastructure, and borders.

Swiss railways

26 cantons by rail

While studying in Geneva, I visited all 26 Swiss cantons, traveling on SBB as well as private, regional, and mountain railways.

Railway publishing

I produced two photo and travel guides on Swiss railways. A total of 100 copies sold out at Comic Market 107.

Publishing

I publish Swiss railway travelogues and photo guides as self-published books. The first title is also available on Kindle in Japanese and English, and I continue to work on railway travel writing.

Japan

I also take long-distance railway trips across Japan, especially on conventional lines. Beyond the trains themselves, I am interested in the regions they serve, urban structure, and transport infrastructure.
