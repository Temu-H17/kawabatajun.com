International experience

Living in Singapore and studying at the University of Geneva gave me experience learning across different institutions, cultures, and languages.

University of Geneva

2024.09 – 2025.06

As an exchange student, I studied Geopolitics, International Organizations, Environmental Diplomacy, and International Environmental Law in English.

Living in Geneva, a major center for international organizations, gave me a perspective on science and technology through policy, diplomacy, and international institutions.

Switzerland

All 26 cantons

During my exchange, I visited all 26 Swiss cantons by rail and documented differences in linguistic regions, cities, transport systems, and local culture.

Japan–Switzerland connection

After returning to Japan, I have continued participating in Swiss-related communities and events and communicating about Switzerland through railway travel writing and other activities.

Languages
