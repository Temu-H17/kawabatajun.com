From sixth grade through high school

I spent seven years in Singapore, from sixth grade through the end of high school, studying at Waseda Shibuya Senior High School in Singapore and living in a multicultural and multilingual environment.

Living abroad was part of everyday life rather than a one-time experience, and it became the foundation for my later interest in studying abroad and international communities.
